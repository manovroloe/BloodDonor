WHO WE ARE

Built around a clear mission, a shared vision, and practical goals.

BloodCare connects donors, people who need blood, staff, administrators, and partner facilities through one coordinated system.

01 Access02 Coordination03 Trust
01

Mission

To make blood donation and blood-request coordination simpler, more accessible, and more organized by connecting donors, patients, staff, and partner facilities through reliable digital workflows and clear information.

Donor supportTimely coordinationReliable records
02

Vision

To support a community where people can quickly understand blood availability, donors can participate with confidence, and partner facilities can coordinate responsibly when blood is needed.

Visible availabilityResponsive communityTrusted partnerships
03

Goal

To improve donor screening and registration, show facility-based blood stock clearly, streamline requests and appointments, strengthen traceability, and keep the public informed through one consistent BloodCare experience.

Clear workflowsBetter traceabilityPublic awareness
