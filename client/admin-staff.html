Staff Accounts

Create real staff login accounts, assign roles, reset passwords, and enable or disable portal access.

Create Staff Login

BloodCare creates the Supabase Authentication user and links the staff profile automatically. Passwords are never stored in the staff table.

Checking Supabase administrator authentication configuration…
Staff are limited to their assigned facility by default. Additional branches can be granted after creation.

Staff Login Flow

1. Admin creates the staff account here with email and password.

2. Supabase Auth securely stores the login credential and BloodCare links the role.

3. Staff logs in from the normal BloodCare Login page and is redirected to the Staff Portal.

Disabled accounts are blocked from signing in until an administrator enables them again.

Authorized Accounts

Use Manage Facilities to control which branches a staff account can operate. Admin accounts remain system-wide but still have a home branch.
